About

A collective, not a brand machine.

Palmetto Music Group is a songwriting collective rooted at Palmetto Baptist Church in Greenville, South Carolina. We write so the church can sing the truth together.

What we believe about worship

Worship is the church’s glad response to the God who has spoken. Songs should carry Scripture’s weight without burying the congregation. Melodies should be learnable on a Sunday morning. Production should serve the room, not replace it.

We write for Palmetto Baptist first. If the songs travel to other churches, we want the resources to travel cleanly with them.

Licensing

Can our church sing these songs?

Yes. Report usage through CCLI when a number is listed.

What does Palmetto Baptist get for free?

Staff and team members attached to the church organization receive complimentary downloads after signing in.

Do individuals need an organization?

No. Anyone can create an account and purchase files.
