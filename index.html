Who we are

A collective, rooted in a local church.

Palmetto Music Group is a songwriting collective at Palmetto Baptist Church in Greenville, South Carolina. We write from the ordinary life of one congregation — rehearsals, Sundays, and the long work of helping people sing what is true.

Our mission

To write Christ-centered songs Palmetto Baptist can sing with confidence — true to Scripture, simple enough for Sunday, and offered with care to the wider church.

More of our story

What we’re about

The heart of the songs.

These are not songs for a stage show. They are written so a room of ordinary believers can lift one voice — with words that carry weight, and melodies a church can learn.

Singable

Melodies that sit in the congregation’s range. Lines a church can learn on a Sunday morning and still mean on a Wednesday night.

Biblical

Words that stay close to Scripture — the gospel named plainly, the church addressed honestly, Christ held at the center.

For the gathered church

Written first for Palmetto Baptist on Sunday. If they travel, they travel as congregational songs, not as a catalog to browse.
